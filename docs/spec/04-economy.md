# 04 — Economy, food and water

## Resources (base)

| Resource | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Scrap metal | Salvage, wrecks, rubble | Walls, tools, machines |
| Wood | Dead trees, salvage | Early building, fuel |
| Stone | Mining | Walls, stoves |
| Cloth | Salvage, fiber crops | Beds, clothes, bandages |
| Components | Rare salvage, crafting (tier 2) | Machines, weapons |
| Coal | Underground | Fuel, steel |
| Steel | Smelting scrap + coal | Better machines, walls |
| Fuel | Old tanks, refinery (tier 3) | Generators, vehicles |
| Chemicals | Salvage, chem bench | Medicine, explosives, fertilizer |
| Electronics | Electronics bench (tier 2+) | Automation, robots |
| Old-world parts | Expeditions only | Key research nodes, story |

## Production chains (examples)

- Scrap → (scrap bench) → scrap tools / scrap walls.
- Scrap + coal → (smelter) → steel.
- Steel + components → (machine shop) → gears, pipes, motors.
- Motors + electronics → (assembler) → conveyors, crafters, robots.
- Crops → (stove) → meals. Meals → better mood.

## Food

| Food | Quality | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Canned food (pre-war) | Simple | From salvage. Does not rot. Limited |
| Raw crop | Raw | Mood penalty |
| Raw meat (mutant) | Raw | Big mood penalty, disease risk |
| Simple meal | Simple | Stove |
| Fine meal | Fine | Good cook + 2 ingredients |
| Nutrient paste | Awful | Late machine. Very efficient. Survivors hate it |
| Booze (moonshine) | Drug | Recreation, addiction risk |

- Food rots by time and temperature. Fridges and cold rooms slow it.
- Food poisoning chance from cook skill and room cleanliness.

## Farming

- Crops: glowcorn (fast, low yield), scrub potato (cold-safe), rust-berry (mood food), fiber weed (cloth), med-root (medicine), tox-lily (chemicals).
- Growth depends on soil, light, temperature and water.
- Hydroponics (tier 2): indoor, powered, no soil needed. Safe from radiation.

## Water

- Water types: **dirty**, **clean**, **contaminated** (radioactive).
- Sources: dirty pools, rain collector (clean unless rain is acid or radioactive), well (clean, may become contaminated), water pump (tier 2), purifier (tier 2, makes clean from dirty or contaminated).
- Water is stored as an item (jugs/barrels) in early game, as a fluid network (tanks + pipes) from tier 2.
- Drinking dirty water: disease chance. Contaminated water: rad dose.

## Radiation cloud event

- Storyteller event. Duration: a few hours to days in-game.
- During the cloud: all survivors outside take rad dose per tick.
- **Contamination**: when the cloud ends, each of the following has a **chance** to become *contaminated* for **one season**:
  - Crops (and seeds sown later in open soil that season).
  - Food and water stacks.
  - Water sources (pools as one roll, rain collectors, wells).
- The chance depends on shelter:

  | Shelter | Radiation cloud | Toxic rain |
  |---|---|---|
  | Open sky (no roof) | 60% | 30% |
  | Roofed, a door opens straight outside | 20% | 8% |
  | Sealed (roof + airlock: two doors in a row, the outer one between walls; or a door into a closed hall) | 0% | 0% |

- **Toxic rain** (weather, after day 3): small rad dose outside, rain collectors give contaminated water, and the same chance rolls when it stops, for **half a season**.
- Items in **sealed rooms** stay clean. This is the main reason to build sealed storage.
- Contaminated food/water can be eaten in desperation (rad dose) or cleaned (purifier, decon shower for items — tier 2).
- Warning: storyteller gives a short alert (half a day) before most clouds. Hard storytellers sometimes do not.

## Demo subset

Scrap, wood, stone, cloth, components, steel, coal. Canned food, raw crops, simple meal. Glowcorn, scrub potato, fiber weed. Dirty pool, rain collector, well, jugs. Radiation cloud with contamination for one season. Rot by temperature (simple).
