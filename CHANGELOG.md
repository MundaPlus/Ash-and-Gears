# Changelog

## 2026-10-09
- Walls join with the walls and doors next to them. A wall with a wall below hides its front face, so vertical walls read as one solid wall (`packages/data/src/art/index.ts` `wallVariant`, `packages/client/src/render/textures.ts`, `world.ts`; neighbors redraw in `packages/sim/src/game.ts`) — the user said vertical walls looked strange.
- An airlock counts only when the outer door sits between two walls (`packages/sim/src/rooms.ts`, tests in `food.test.ts` and `tests/e2e/roof.spec.ts`). The showcase house has such an airlock now (`packages/sim/src/showcase.ts`) — the user asked for walls beside the second door.
- Salvage is split in two orders. Salvage takes only ruins, wrecks and junk, so a big drag no longer tears down your own walls. Deconstruct takes down your own buildings (`packages/sim/src/designations.ts`, `map.ts`, `packages/client/src/ui/Toolbar.tsx`, `render/textures.ts`) — found in a Chrome play session.
- A warning when plans wait for a material the colony does not have, and the tile info of a plan lists what it still needs (`packages/sim/src/construction.ts`, `packages/client/src/main.ts`). The tile info title shows the floor name on floored tiles. Added the "Building a roof" task label (`packages/data/i18n/en.json`) — found in the same play session.
- Roofs are no longer automatic. An enclosed room needs a roof, and survivors build it tile by tile as construction work. A faint blue hatch marks tiles that wait for a roof. A roof falls in when its room opens. New maps, the showcase and old saves get their roofs at once. Saves are now version 2 and store roofs (`packages/sim/src/rooms.ts`, `construction.ts`, `map.ts`, `game.ts`, `save.ts`, `showcase.ts`, `packages/client/src/render/world.ts`) — the user asked for survivors to build the roof.
- Radiation clouds contaminate only by chance: 60% under the open sky, 20% in a roofed room with a door straight to outside, 0% in a sealed room. A room is sealed when it has a full roof and an airlock (two doors in a row, or a door into a closed hall). New toxic rain weather (after day 3) gives a small dose outside and dirty rain water, with 30% / 8% / 0% chances for half a season. The tile info shows the shelter state (`packages/sim/src/weather.ts`, `farming.ts`, `packages/client/src/main.ts`, `packages/data/i18n/en.json`) — the user asked for chance-based contamination and a safe double door.
- Tests: chance shares, airlock sealing and roof building (`packages/sim/test/food.test.ts`), and a browser test that builds a room with an airlock through the toolbar (`tests/e2e/roof.spec.ts`).
- Spec 03 and 04 updated for roofs, airlocks and the chance table; spec page rebuilt (`docs/spec/`, `docs/spec.html`).

They are loudness-normalized to 128 kbps MP3 (7.9 MB in total) in `packages/client/public/music/`. `packages/client/src/audio.ts` picks a track by mood, time of day and tech tier, crossfades between tracks and loops by crossfade. The synthesized music stays as a fallback for missing files. The presentation footer now names the music source (`packages/client/public/site/index.html`).
